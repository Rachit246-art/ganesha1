ABOUT US

Celebrating Bappa, Preserving Nature

Founded in 2025, Div Mangal Murtis is committed to making Ganesh Chaturthi more meaningful through beautifully handcrafted 100% eco-friendly clay Ganpati idols. Created by skilled artisans using natural clay and non-toxic colours, every Murti is designed to celebrate tradition while caring for our environment.

Our mission is to help families welcome Lord Ganesha responsibly, ensuring every visarjan returns safely to nature. For those who wish to make their celebration even more meaningful, we also offer an Eco Seed Hamper containing seeds, cocopeat, and a planter kit for an additional ₹500, allowing Bappa's blessings to bloom into a living plant.

Choose devotion that nurtures both your home and the planet.

Our Vision

To inspire every family to celebrate Ganesh Chaturthi with devotion, sustainability, and timeless craftsmanship creating traditions that protect nature for generations to come.
